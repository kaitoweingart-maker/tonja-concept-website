About Tonja

Where design becomes a celebration.

TONJA Concept is a curated design and fashion concept store — a playful universe where colour, craft and character come first. Every piece is hand-picked: maximalist ceramics, statement fashion, art and objects you won't find anywhere else.

From our home in Basel to the mountains of Gstaad and the heart of Geneva, we bring together the bold and the beautiful — and soon, Zürich joins the family.
